# Chapter 1 icons: meat, pelt, mushroom, angelica, dishes (Colin, AD, 2026-10-29)

Clive's chapter 1 asks 2 and 5, plus the icons from 3 and 4. Nine icons, each 160 by 160, in the bag's look (the same builder as meat,
stew and the fish). Copy `icons/*.png` into `assets/sprites/icons/`.

- `boarMeat`: a thick dark red cut under a deep rind of fat, with the skin's brown edge.
- `venison`: a lean, dark haunch with a long bone and little fat.
- `wolfPelt`: a grey pelt with a dark saddle, the bushy tail with its dark tip, and the head's shape with ears.
- `mushroom`: three pale caps on thin stems from one root.
- `angelica`: a green stalk with reddish rings, a round umbel of green-white flowers, and a leaf.
- `roastVenison`: the haunch roasted brown with two sprigs of herb.
- `boarStew`: an iron pot on three legs with a bail handle, holding boar, mushroom and berries in a brown stew.
- `venisonBerries`: venison slices and lingonberries on a wooden board.
- `mushroomBroth`: a bowl of pale broth with small brown mushroom caps and angelica leaves.

`icons_beside.png` shows them beside meat, cookedMeat, hide and stew. `item_icons.py` is the builder with the new kinds.
