## The race in one belief (`being.trolls`)

**A troll never stops growing.** Trolls are the mountain's own children, made from the stone as men were made from trees, and kin
to the giants. A troll grows as long as it lives and lives as long as its hill. So the kinds are one people at different ages, and
**the big ones are deep because they are old**: the old go down, away from the sun and nearer the stone.

- **Kin.** Households: a mother who decides, a father who is strong, old ones, young ones. The mothers rule, and the brutes do as
  they say.
- **Speech.** They speak slowly in an older tongue; the house trolls have learnt ours. They keep a bargain to the letter, love a
  riddle, count badly past three, and never give their names.
- **They keep** ore, silver, gold, travellers' bones, cattle under the hill, and sometimes people (real: *bergtagning*).
- **They fear** the sun (stone), church bells, thunder (Thor), steel thrown over them, and a little, fire.

## The Trollvinir

Their trolls are house trolls, Gríma's friend among them. The pact is with the households, and the mothers keep the brutes to it.
The jutuls bargain and teach (the Trollvinir's "old knowledge"). What walks below keeps no pact; the Trollvinir never go below the
third stair.

## Your three open questions, as the canon answers them

1. **Do all trolls turn to stone in daylight?** In the lore, yes, by Robin's own words: 2026-10-05 ("a troll caught in daylight
   turns to stone where it stands") and 2026-10-21 (the Trollvinir: "no other troll in the world has seen the day and lived"). The
   deep ones never meet it. What is a rule in play is yours and Robin's.
2. **Can the house trolls be friends?** Yes. The Trollvinir are friends with trolls (Robin, 2026-10-21), and Hårungen leave you be
   unless struck (Robin, 2026-10-16).
3. **Which kinds belong to chapter 2's north?** The trolls live in chapter 2's north (Robin, 2026-10-26), and the lore fits every
   kind there except the Gape, which is the west's. Which are built is Robin's.
