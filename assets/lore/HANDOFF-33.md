## For the game now: the shadows, never named

### `rumour.sea.shadows` (rumour, whisper, villagers and fishers of home water, variants)

1. A shadow went under my boat last summer. Longer than the boat, and in no hurry.
2. Don't look over the side too long. Sometimes the dark down there is moving.
3. Under the ice you see them go by in the black patches. Slow. Never stopping.
4. Out here we don't name what's under us. A name's how a thing finds you.

Line 4 is real: northern fishermen would not say certain words at sea (the haaf words of Shetland, the Norwegian noa-names). It
is why the shadows have no name. Folk disagree about the big one (a whale, the Hafgufa asleep, a coil of the Serpent); the game
decides, and the lore never says.

### `line.fisherman.shadows` (Gamli, variants)

When: Gamli talked to after the hero has seen a great shadow pass under the boat or the ice. When exactly is yours.

1. Saw one go under you? Then you know why I fish the shallows.
2. Sixty years. I've seen the big one twice. Both times I rowed home and sat a while.
3. Most of them are whales. Most. Keep rowing, and don't stop to see which.

## For chapter 2, proposed (the lore under Colin, AD's boards)

- **The pirates: the Wends** (`world.wends`, `god.four-faced`, `place.white-cliff-fort`, `rumour.wends.horse`).
  - Slavic sea-folk of the sand spits and lagoons on the far south-east shore: raiders, slavers and traders in low, fast ships.
  - Before a raid, their four-faced god answers through a white horse led over spears. A third of the plunder is his.
  - Strongholds: a temple-fort on white cliffs; lagoon towns behind shifting channels; a sea-fort with its harbour inside the
    walls.
  - Threats: sand bars, fog, archers in the reeds, stakes in the channels, false fires, slavers, the drowned one in the lagoons.
  - The Curonians and Oeselians are further east, for later chapters.
  - A proposal for the south: they are why the villages stopped paying their jarl. He took tribute and did not keep the Wends off.
- **The tropic islands: the Warm Isles** (`place.warm-isles`, `place.sun-temple`, `thing.sea-bean`, `rumour.warm-isles`).
  - No villages. Stepped temples in the forest, every carved face with its eyes shut, every door facing north.
  - Folk say the sun sleeps there in winter. Who built the temples, folk disagree: giants, the elves of Freyr's Álfheimr, or
    the deathless of the Deathless Acre.
  - Threats: the heat, fever, snakes, a river-thing like a ship's ram, a great cat in the branches, a mountain that smokes.
  - **Open for Robin** (question 65): how they lie within reach. My pick: far south, toward Muspell's heat, the sea warming as you
    go, with a warm current as the sea-road. The sea bean is a real find that comes up that current.
