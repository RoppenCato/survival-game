# The troll's traces in the wolves' cave (Colin, AD, 2026-10-29)

Clive's chapter 1 ask 6: the troll is gone and the wolves have taken his cave. Copy `sheets/*` into a set folder (for example
`assets/props/trolltrace/`). The set pieces carry the pieces' thin line.

**Reuse from the cave kit, as they are:** `bonepile_1` (a moose skull, ribs and long bones) and `bonepile_2` (the same with a broken
shield). They are the troll's leavings. Don't use `trollBed` here: it is his bed while he lived, green moss and fresh furs. The column,
stalagmite and fungus pieces fit any cave.

**New pieces (`trolltrace.json`):**
- `trollBedOld` (4 m): his old bed, a heap of matted rotten straw with the hollow he lay in, dark moss and a torn grey fur dragged half
  off. The straw is strewn by the wolves, and gnawed bones lie in it.
- `gnawedBones_1`: a femur near as long as a man, gnawed to ragged stumps with dark tooth holes, a smaller bone across it, and chips.
- `gnawedBones_2`: a great rib cage split open over its spine, a long bone gnawed at one end, and an ox's skull on its side.
- `coldHearth` (3.4 m): ten knee-high boulders in a ring, blackened on the fire side, grey ash, four log ends burnt to black stumps,
  and the great spit fallen across the ring. No glow: long cold.

**Claw marks (`claws.json`, decals with no ink ring, anchored at their middle):**
- `clawMarks_floor`: four deep gouges across the floor, about 1.1 m long, foreshortened as the ground is.
- `clawMarks_wall`: four gouges raked down a wall, for a wall face.
- Each gouge has a pale scraped lip toward the light and a dark floor. Lay them flat, under everything, at the floor's draw order.

`look.png` shows them on the cave floor with the hero for size.

— Colin, AD
