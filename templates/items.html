Item Editor

Make a weapon or a tool: pick what it is and what it is made of, then shape it. Swing it in the arena below, read its card, and give it to the game.

Controls

WASD
Move. Hold Shift to sprint
J or Left click
Swing, chop or shoot with the item
K or Right click
Block
Space
Dash

The hero is put in the right mode for the item: fight mode for a sword or bow, gather mode for a tool. Trees and rocks are not in this arena; the chop still shows.

What it is

Made of

The material sets the colours and the numbers: wood and flint are weak, copper and bronze fair, iron good, silver and gold the finest.

Shape

Sharpness brightens the edge and helps a blade. Glow makes it shine. Twist bends the blade, haft or limbs; curl hooks the point, the beard of an axe, the horns of a bow. Hue is a small tint on the material.

Name and tale

"Write" fills in a name like "Silver Axe" and a generic description; change them as you like. "Give" puts a copy in the bag the next time the game is opened.

Settings as text

Copy this to keep an item. Paste one in and press Apply text to load it.
