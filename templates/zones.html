World Prototype

A new random world every time. You start in the great hall of a huge abandoned Factory, with everything outside dark. Explore the halls, smash crates, barrels, chairs and tables, and find the key to the great gate. Then step out into the huge Commons, full of places that would make good bases. Press New world to roll a different layout.
