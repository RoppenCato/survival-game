Survival game workbench

One engine and one set of numbers. The game is played by chapter; each system has a place of its own to try it in, and what is tuned there is what the game runs on.

Play

The game as it is played. H opens Admin: cheats, the season, the weather, the hour, and every place by name.

Chapters 0 and 1The gameFrom the wreck with nothing: the starter island, Arnor and his boat, then the big island, its villages and the islands round. The name screen offers a start from either chapter.

Try a system

Each a part of the game set up to be tried by itself, on the game's own engine. In the four scenes, H opens the board of choices.

FightingCombat ArenaAn empty ring. Bring men, hirdmen and beasts; pick your weapon and your runes from tiles; slow the clock and see every blow drawn and written down. Every combat number as a slider. RaidingRaid EditorOne of every kind of place, from a farmstead to a town. Choose the place, its men, your crew and the way in: from your ship, before its gate, unarmed as a guest, or by night. Breaking and burningDestruction EditorA sampler of every wall and roof, a town and a village, fire and wind, mending. Every number of the rules as a slider. RunesRune EditorThe altar with a boar on it and the season due: E makes the sacrifice and the storm brings stones to choose from. Bind runes and try them on a boar or two men. The mountainMountain testA mountain island of its own with a way to every place on it: the slopes, the plateau, the peaks, the cave, the old mine and the sea cave.

Look and make

How things look and how places are made. Each hands its result to the game with "Use in the game".

ArtModelsEvery figure on the game’s own island, drawn by the game: the hero in his gear, the people, the beasts, the farm animals. Walk as one, play its clips, see its sheet. ArtSet piecesEvery picture the studio made for the world, by its set, on the game’s ground at the game’s size with its shadow and footprint. PlacesVillage EditorA place of any size grown by one grammar, from the farmstead to the great town, with its ways in, its secrets and its men’s posts. PlacesSea EditorThe islands and the sea between, the ships and their handling, the crossing to the big island. Later chaptersWorld monstersThe kraken on the game’s water: its field of arms, the head rising, the ship between them. Not in the game yet.
