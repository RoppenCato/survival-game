Survival game workbench

One engine, one set of rules, many doors into it. The game is played by chapter; the systems that every chapter shares (fighting, raiding, destruction, the mountains) each have a place of their own to work on them, and whatever is tuned there is what the chapters run on. The editors change how things look and how places are made.

Editors

How things look and how places are made. Each hands its result to the game with "Use in the game".

Art EditorModelsEvery figure on the game’s own island, drawn by the game: the hero dressed as the game dresses him, the people, the beasts and the farm animals, the trolls and the väsen waiting for their chapter. Walk as one, play its clips, see its sheet. Art EditorSet piecesEvery picture the studio made for the world, by its set, with its variants and states, on the game’s ground at the game’s size with its shadow and footprint; the things still drawn in code with their tuner. Village EditorA place of any size grown by one grammar, from the farmstead to the great town: a tier, a seed, the wealth, the shore, the shape and the story; seen from above or zoomed into, with the ways in, the secrets and the men's posts drawn over it. Sea EditorThe islands and the sea between: sizes and distances, the villages planned on them, the ships and their handling, the Shipyard.
