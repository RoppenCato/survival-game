Combat Arena

The fight on its own, on the game's own engine: what is tuned here is what the game and the raids fight with (src/combat.js, the weapons' numbers in docs/weapons.md, the men's in MEN). Beasts and men against you, hirdmen beside you, every weapon, the shield and the gear, the clock slowed, the blows drawn and written down. Everything works from the keyboard; the mouse only aims the bow.

The last blows


        

        
What landed on whom and what it cost, newest first: a dodge (a blow that came during a dash), a parry, a block and what got through, a hit on a man's shield, a guard broken, a kill. The clock is the arena's own seconds.

      

      

        
Controls

        

          
WASD
Move. Hold Shift to sprint (it drains stamina)

          
J or Left click
Attack: a chain of blows, the last one harder. Hold it to charge a heavy blow, let go to strike

          
K or Right click
Hold to guard. Raised in the instant a blow lands it is a parry. With a shield on the arm the guard is wide and strong and stops arrows; without, you only fend

          
Space
Dash in the direction you move (a backstep standing still): a moment untouchable, and through a foe to his back

          
F
Swap between the melee weapon and the ranged one. With the bow out the attack button shoots

          
G
Your men: tapped, they break off and come to you; held, they hold a line where you stand

          
Ctrl
Target the nearest foe; again for the next, then to let go

          
Q 1 2 3
Gather mode and its tools (axe, pick, knife): tools do not hurt anybody

          
H
Add a wolf or a boar

          
R
Rise again after falling

They come

        

        

        

          
          
        

        
A man fights as in the game: he shows his blow coming, keeps his weapon's reach, guards with his shield if he has one, yields or runs when it goes badly. Your men wear your red; theirs carry blue shields. They come at a man by the way chosen (one at a time by default, as the game runs).

      


      

        
Beasts

        

        
Each fights its own way (ATTACKS in src/art.js): the boar charges past, the wolves ring you and bite one at a time, the bear rears and comes down with both paws, the adder's bite poisons, the moose gores. The trolls sweep slow and hard. The huldra, Näcken and the bysen do not fight.

      


      

        
Tuning

        
The numbers that decide how a fight feels, live: what you move here is what the next blow does. A number you have moved is green. Use in the game hands them to the game (it reads them when it loads); Take back brings back what the game has now.
