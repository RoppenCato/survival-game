Combat Arena

Fight the animals of the first islands: wolves, boars, snakes and the bear. Everything works from the keyboard alone. The mouse is only used to aim the ranged weapon, and you can switch that to auto.

A crit hits for 50% more, shows in yellow, and rattles the enemy: it moves and attacks 30% slower for a moment.

Options

Weapons

F swaps between the melee weapon and the ranged one. Each fights its own way (docs/weapons.md): the arc, reach and pace of a hit, how many chain, and what sets it apart. Thrown things are endless here; in the game they lie where they fall.

Arena

Controls

WASD
Move
J or Left click
Attack (three-hit combo, the third hits harder). You can keep moving while you swing
Hold J or Left click
Charge a heavy swing for up to a second, then let go. Dash or block to cancel
K or Right click
Hold to block. Press just before a hit to parry
Hold Shift
Sprint while moving. It drains stamina and stops when you run out
Space
Dash. A quick sidestep in the direction you move (a backstep if you stand still), with brief invulnerability. You dash through enemies
Q
Switch between fight and gather mode
F
Fight mode: swap between the melee weapon and the ranged one. With the bow out, the attack button shoots (at the mouse cursor, or automatically)
Ctrl
Target the nearest enemy; press again for the next nearest, then to let go
1 to 3
Gather mode: axe, pickaxe, knife. The right tool does full damage, the wrong one half
H
Add a wolf or a boar
R
Restart after you are scrapped

Try these: hold the attack button for a charged swing, keep moving while you swing, dash through a wolf to get behind it, parry a boar's charge, and fill the bear's stagger bar to stun it. Then turn Juice off to feel the stiff version of the same combat.
