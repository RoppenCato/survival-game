Light Editor

Lighting for a flat world, each technique a switch: shadows cast by the sun and stretching with the hour, the colour of the hour over everything, sun rays, a lit rim on every figure and prop, the fire’s light at night with its own shadows, and form on the trolls’ heads. Drag the hour, or let it run.

Each button turns one technique on or off, so they can be judged one at a time and together. The sun stands upper left at noon, as the art direction asks; at morning the shadows lean west, at evening east, and at night only the fire casts them.

Cast shadows

Every prop and figure throws its own silhouette onto the ground, skewed by the sun and stretched by the hour; a cool blue-black, soft at the edge. The biggest single step toward the light of a 3D game that a flat one can take.

The colour of the hour

A tint over the whole frame: warm and low at morning, clear at noon, amber and rose at evening, blue at night; and the vignette.

Light on things

The rim is a pale edge on the lit side of every figure and prop. The sun rays are soft wedges from the upper left through the day. The fire lights its circle at night and throws the shadows of what stands near it away from itself. Head form lights the trolls’ heads as round things: a highlight upper left, the brow’s shadow over the eyes, a shadow beside the nose, the jaw turning under.

Settings as text

Use in the game stores these numbers (game.light). The game takes the head form now; the shadows and the colour of the hour are wired in next.
