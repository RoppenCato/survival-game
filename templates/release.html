SKARGARD

__VERSION__ · a test build

__NOTES__

Play

The game saves itself in this browser as you play. To carry it to another computer: Esc, Save to a file; on the other computer, Load from a file.

Keys

WASD
Walk; Shift sprints, Space dashes, C sneaks
J / left click
Swing what is in hand
K / right click
Guard (a parry if just in time); with the bow, draw it
E
Use what you face: talk, take, open, sleep, board
1–6
Take a thing in hand or use it
Tab
Your gear, the bag and what you can make
B
Build, once you know how
L O M
The book, your own half of it, the map
Esc
The menu: the save, music and sound

An early test, made to be played and broken. Best on a computer with a keyboard and mouse, in Chrome or Edge.
