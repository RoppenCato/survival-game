Art Editor

Everything that is drawn or modelled, in one place: the Blender models (the hero), the drawn creatures and the world's objects. One arena, one engine; the tabs change what stands in it and what the panels on the right tune. The hero in every tab is the Blender model; the old drawn hero is kept in art/backup.

Creature

Saved creatures

In the game

Save keeps the design under its name in this browser; New creature starts a variation from what is on screen; Update in game makes the chosen beast of the game look and fight like this design (the game keeps its own counts, damage and drops); Take back restores the game's own.

Model

The figure's size on the ground, here and in the game (game.blenderScale): 1 is the render's own size, which since 2026-10-09 is the drawn hero's height for the Mixamo sheets (the older sheets render larger: 0.85 fits them).

In the hand

The things modelled for the hand (2026-10-09): each is its wood and its metal as layers over the body, the metal tinted by the material in the game; here iron. They show in the clips they were rendered for.

Gear

Each piece is its own layer, rendered over the body as a holdout, so they stack in any order.

Scene

Zoom enlarges the drawing only: at 1 the figure is the game's size. The starter island is the game's own world (one island of 60 tiles with the wreck) drawn as the game draws it; the night forest is the kit's props in the Night Forest key with one campfire. New sheets: npm run art:mixamo:sheets, then npm run build.
