Art Direction

The same scene twice: above as the game draws it now, below in the new look from docs/art-direction.md: Egerkrans’s ink line (thin toward the light, heavy on the shadow side), Bauer’s light (one sun, cool blue-grey shadows, warm edges), grain in every fill, a muted earth palette. The ink look is the game’s look now; the sliders retune it, and Use in the game hands a retuned version over (Take back returns to the defaults).
