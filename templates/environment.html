Environment Editor

Build a patch of the Viking world and walk through it. Pick a biome, choose the ground and which objects grow there, then steer how dense, how grouped and how open it is. The aim is groves and clearings with room to breathe, and room to build a base.

Open ground is every spot with no tree, rock or landmark close by. A base plot is a free rectangle of 6 by 5 tiles.
