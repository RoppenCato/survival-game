Song Editor

The game's music, played by its own instruments. Pick a song and hear it, change the pace and the instruments, or start a new one and let the dice find a tune. Save what you like to the library; the game plays only what you hand it with "Use in the game".

Try something

New song makes a fresh one from the dice: chords, a melody, a harp, a bass and a shaker. New tune keeps the chords and rolls another melody. The dice stay in the key and on the chord, so it always fits; roll until something sticks.

Keep it

The library is kept in this browser. Built-in songs cannot be deleted; saving one makes your own copy.

Tracks

Each track: its instrument, how it plays (a written melody, or a pattern from the chords), how loud, when it plays (peace, fight, or both), and on or off.

Song

Advanced opens the piano roll, the chords and the notes as text, and the song as text.

Chords, one per bar

Notes of track

Choose a track with its number button. Notes are name and octave, a colon, and a length: w h q e s, dotted with a point; r is a rest; + joins a chord; @70 sets how hard.
