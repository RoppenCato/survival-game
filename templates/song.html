Song Editor

The game's music, played by its own instruments. Pick a song, hear it, change tempo, key and the tracks, and write new melodies in the note text. The game plays whatever is saved here. Export a .mid to open the song in any DAW.

Peace plays the calm layer, Danger the fight layer over the shared bones of the song, as the game crossfades them when a beast wakes. Click in the roll to jump.

Tracks

A melody track is written as notes: F5:q. E5:e r:e C5:e (name and octave, then length w h q e s, a dot for dotted, r rests, + stacks a chord, @60 sets a velocity). Bars with | are for reading only. A pattern track plays from the chords: arp, pad, bass, comp, pulse, and the drums shaker, rim, kick.

Song

Chords, one per bar

F, Dm7, Bb, C7, Fmaj7, Gsus, Bdim, F6, F/A. The patterns follow these; the melodies do not move with them, so change both.
