The game

The first island. You land with nothing: pick up branches and stones at camp and make your first tools by hand, chop and mine, build a hut, put a workbench under its roof, hunt the boar for meat and hide, and come back to it all later: the game saves itself. Esc has restart and a sandbox.

Controls

Walk. Hold Shift to sprint
Q
Switch between fight mode and gather mode
J or Left click
Swing the sword or the tool, or shoot the bow. Hold for a charged swing
K or Right click
Block. Press just before a hit to parry
Space
Dash
F
Fight mode: swap sword and bow
1 2 3
Gather mode: axe, pickaxe, knife. The right tool does full damage, the wrong one half
Ctrl
Target the nearest beast; again for the next, then let go
Esc
The menu: continue, a sandbox to try things out in, restart with nothing, a new island, the music volume and the evening theme
B
The board: Building, Crafting (made by hand, anywhere) and Workbench (only with a roof over it). Recipes show as ? until you have picked up every kind of thing they need. Build mode. Right click opens the board of pieces: pick one, then click to place it (drag for walls and floors). F turns the cursor into the wreck cursor: the piece under it glows red, and a click takes it down and gives the material back
Mouse wheel
Step through the hotbar: tools in gather mode, pieces in build mode, the belt in fight mode
E
At a hearth: the board on its Hearth tab, to cook. At a bed, from dusk: sleep until morning. At a chest: open it beside the bag (click moves things across). At Brokk: talk
Right click on a thing
In the bag: equip it (a sword to the weapon slot, an axe to the axe slot). On the character panel: take it off into the bag. Drag a thing off the boards to drop it on the ground; walk over it to pick it up again
Tab
The character panel slides in from the left (the hero, the equipment slots, and the quiver: three arrow slots, click one to shoot from it) and the bag from the right. The bag: a wooden board that slides in from the right. Six slots wide, four high, stacks of 25. The top row is the belt and matches the hotbar numbers. Drag to move things, click food to eat it, Sort tidies the rows below the belt
R
Rise again after you have fallen

What there is to do

Trees take a few chops with the axe and fall. Rocks crack as you mine them and break apart. Bushes are cut with the knife. What falls out lies on the ground until you walk over it, and lands in your inventory.

The bow shoots from the quiver: you start with twenty wood arrows, and most arrows that miss land on the ground and can be picked up again. Making more arrows comes with the workbench.

Six kinds of beast live on the island, each on its own ground: boars and deer in the open, adders on the grass by the beach, wolves in pairs in the dark forest, and far from camp, in the deepest woods, moose and a bear. They keep to their home ranges and leave you alone until you come close; the deer runs, the rest charge. Meat and hide from most; antler from the moose. Berry bushes give berries.

Somewhere inland is a broken steading: a burnt hall, a hut still standing, a stone house and a palisade with gaps, crates with things left behind (break them open with the axe), and Brokk the smith by the well, who will tell you what happened. The pieces are the same you build with, and the wreck cursor takes them apart for their wood and stone. A chest you build holds twelve stacks.

Food never punishes you: you do not have to eat. Eat, and you heal; cook a meal at the hearth and your health keeps coming back for minutes, with your top health raised. Roast boar is good; boar and berry stew is better. Open the bag and click food to eat it, or put food on the belt and press its number.

Building is cheap: a wall, door or window costs two wood (a stone wall two stone), a floor tile one wood, and a hearth of your own four stone and two wood (you can cook at it like the one at camp). Close a loop of walls and a turf roof appears by itself, and fades when you step inside. Add a door, or you will not get in.

Your island, inventory, buildings and felled trees are saved in this browser and are there when you come back. "New island" throws it all away and starts again.

The chart in the corner shows the island; the yellow dot is you.
