Base Editor

Build a Viking homestead piece by piece. Lay floors, raise walls along the edges of tiles, add doors and windows, and put furniture inside. When walls, doors and windows close a loop, a roof appears by itself and fades away when you walk in.

Controls

Left click or drag
Place the chosen piece
Right click or drag
Remove what is under the cursor (pieces, furniture, trees, rocks)
WASD
Walk. Hold Shift to sprint. The view follows you
J
Attack

Piece

Wall material

Floor

Furniture

Roof

A roof covers every space that is fully closed in by walls, doors and windows. Each room gets its own, so only the room you stand in opens up.
