Village Editor

One grammar from the farmstead to the great town (docs/settlements-plan.md): a focus, a spine, units grown round it, the edge drawn last, the outside; over it the ways in, the secrets and the defence. Pick a tier, roll a seed, set the wealth, the shore, the shape, the story and the age; the audit says what the rules ask and what the place gives. The wheel zooms, the left button drags the view, a double click fits it. "Use in the game" hands the numbers to the game's villages.
