Gathering Editor

Chopping, mining and cutting as a small game: a field of trees, rocks and bushes to work with the axe, the pick and the knife. Turn each idea on or off and tune it until it feels right, then hand the settings to the game.

Controls

WASD
Move. Hold Shift to sprint
1 2 3
Axe, pickaxe, knife (you start in gather mode; Q swaps to fight)
J or Left click
Swing. Hold to keep swinging; tap in time for clean hits. With the heavy blow rune: hold back, let go
Space
Dash, out of the way of a falling tree

Presets

Plain is the game as it was; Everything turns every idea on. The sliders then change it.

Trees

A felled tree falls away from you. If it fells, the trees it lands on come down too. If it hurts, standing where it lands costs health. With trunks, it lies as a trunk to chop into logs. Nests: now and then eggs, or honey with angry bees.

Rocks

How the soft facet shows

A rock is soft on one facet: a blow from that side bites deeper. The cue is drawn on the rock: the crack itself, a pale vein, a patch of moss, or the sunny side (always the left, as for trees: stand on the sunny side and strike).

Aiming and rhythm

How a tree's sweet side shows

A tree has a sweet side: a blow from there is a critical hit, its number gold and large. The cue: a glint, a scar on the bark, the tree leaning toward it, or the sunny side (always the left, the side the light falls on; no cue to look for, just stand right). The glint and the scar wander now and then if you let them. Rhythm: a blow landed just as the last one's recoil settles is a clean hit (a large number); holding the button misses the window, tapping in time finds it. Swing pace sets how fast the tool swings.

Toughness and regrowth

Stumps grow back after some days as saplings. Next day moves the field's clock.
