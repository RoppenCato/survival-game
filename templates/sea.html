Sea Editor

Walk down the jetty, board the ship and sail between islands. Pick a ship, then change how it handles and how the sea looks. Step ashore anywhere the boat touches land.

The chart in the corner shows the whole sea. The white dot is the boat, the yellow dot is you.

Controls

WASD
Walk on land, steer on water. Hold Shift to sprint on land
E
Board the boat when you stand next to it. Step ashore when the boat is next to land

Boat

Choosing a boat sets its handling. The sliders then change it.

Steering

Glide is how long the boat keeps moving after you let go. Grip is how firmly it follows its bow; low grip lets it slide sideways in turns.

Sea

Archipelago

Sizes and gaps are in tiles: 100 tiles is one kilometre, and the hero is about one tile wide. Island width is the normal island; Size variety mixes in small and large ones. Most gaps are near the typical gap; Gap variety adds narrow straits and the occasional long crossing. Skerries are the tiny rocky islets around the coasts.
