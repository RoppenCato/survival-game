Sea Editor

Walk down the jetty, board the ship and sail between islands. Pick a ship, then change how it handles and how the sea looks. Step ashore anywhere the boat touches land.

The ship

The game's ships (docs/ships-rework.md): the rowboat, the karve for 4, 8 and 12, the longship for 16, 20 and 26, sailed by the same code as the game (src/yard.js). Choosing one sets its handling and puts its full crew at the oars; the sliders then change them, and Use in the game hands the handling to the game for that ship.

The chart in the corner shows the whole sea. The white dot is the boat, the yellow dot is you.

Controls

WASD
Walk on land, steer on water. Hold Shift to sprint on land
E
Board the ship when you stand next to it. Step ashore when it is next to land

Steering

The game's ship of this size takes this steering, speed, acceleration, turn, glide and grip, and the wind's strength.

The wind is a buff only: a karve's sail with the wind behind goes up to (1 + strength) times its top speed, a wind ahead costs nothing. Each rower adds 7% while the seats last.

Glide is how long the boat keeps moving after you let go. Grip is how firmly it follows its bow; low grip lets it slide sideways in turns.

Sea

The game's world

The game builds its sea from these numbers on its next fresh start (a running save is kept until New island or Restart). The chart shows every village the plan gives this sea, by kind, and where the lowlands begin.

Archipelago

Sizes and gaps are in tiles: 100 tiles is one kilometre, and the hero is about one tile wide. Island width is the normal island; Size variety mixes in small and large ones. Most gaps are near the typical gap; Gap variety adds narrow straits and the occasional long crossing. Skerries are the tiny rocky islets around the coasts.
