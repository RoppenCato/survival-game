Creature Editor

Design an animal or a monster and meet it in the arena. Start from a base model, pick a body plan, then change its features, colours, how it moves and how it warns you before it attacks. The settings can be copied out as text and pasted back in later.

Poses

Standing, moving, warning before an attack, attacking, and stunned.

Arena

Every creature in the arena shows the current design. Hold still freezes them so you can look closely.

Troll

Details

The Troll body plan, after John Bauer: an old boulder of a thing, hunched, with hair down its back gone grey, lichen growing on it, knuckles on the ground, a long nose and great ears. Age greys the hair and adds warts. The moss colour is under Colours.
