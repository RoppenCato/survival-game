# Chapter 1: its goals and subgoals, and what a played run found (2026-11-14)

Robin, 2026-11-14: "go through all of chapter 1, then note down the general goals and subgoals of chapter 1 so we can tweak it a
bit and get it good and balanced. Do it by a playthrough from chapter 1 to end."

**This page waits for Robin.** It says what chapter 1 asks of a player as the game stands, what each step cost in the run, what
is out of balance, and eight questions with my recommendation. Every number is from one run on the game's own world (seed 11).

## How the run was played

- Started from the name screen's **Chapter 1** (the big island's shore, the richer kit).
- Played in the pane by script through the game's own rules: the E rule, the crafting board, the Building board, the engine's
  blows.
- **Rowing and sailing were steered for real.** Fights were fought by a simple fighter (it guards during a wind-up and strikes
  otherwise; a player who parries does better).
- **Walking was timed, not always walked.** My walker gets stuck on fences and walls, so long legs were timed by distance at the
  pace I measured overland (60 units a second; the hero jogs at 78, and trees and rocks cost the rest).
- The error log stayed empty for the whole run.

## Findings

### Faults, fixed during the run

1. **A wolf could not be carried out of the cave.** With a beast on his shoulders, E at the cave's way out said only "Set it
   down". The quest sends you into the cave for a wolf, so this blocked the main goal's own hint. E now says "Leave the cave".
2. **A fall could strand you for good.** Killed on the big island before I had a bed there, the hero woke in Arnor's house
   with his boat across the sea and no way back. The boat now lies at the nearest shore of where he wakes (your rule for dying
   at sea, taken for every fall: mine).
3. **The first summer was six days, not four.** A reset written for your old save ran on every new game. A new game keeps its
   day 2 again. The Chapter 1 start now begins on day 4 of summer (mine), so the blót is two mornings off.
4. **Two villagers had an empty line** among their seven. It is passed over now.

### Balance: the big ones

1. **The main goal and the end are two separate roads, and the end is shorter.** Nothing on the way to the ship asks for the
   blót. In the run the ship sailed on day 4 and the blót came on day 7.
2. **One island gives everything.** The island nearest the landing has the flint, the old pines and the flax within six tiles
   of each other. It is also where Aslak's clue sends you. The elm island, the fisherman's island, the five home islands and
   the mountain are never needed.
3. **The Old Man is not hard.** He has 132 health and hits for 40, but he walks at 44 (the hero jogs at 78). The simple
   fighter killed him in 38 seconds on the first try with a flint spear, full leather and a shield, for 61 health. He stood 170
   tiles from everything worth gathering, so he is never in the way either.
4. **The whole chapter is about an hour.** The ship's own price is small. What takes time is walking, rowing and carrying
   wolves.
5. **Wolf pelts are a carrying chore.** No butcher's table can be built, so every beast goes to a village's table on your
   shoulders. A pelt is four minutes, of which ten seconds is the fight.
6. **The blót is a wait, not a deed.** The wolf was on the altar on day 1. Then nothing to do for it but sleep six times
   (four, now the summer is fixed; two from the Chapter 1 start).
7. **Night can be skipped whole.** With a bed, sleep is offered at dusk, so a player never has to meet a pack. Without one,
   a torch burns six minutes and the night is seven: when it went out the pack of three killed a standing hero with 42 health
   in under ten seconds, with no warning that the torch was dying.
8. **The home has no pull.** A hut of 34 wood does everything. The loom works outdoors, weaving takes seconds, and Arnor only
   arrives after the blót.

### Smaller things

9. **The first village you meet is a blank wall.** Dagnysnes has a dry-stone wall with no gate on the landing's side, only a
   back gate on the far side. Its five merchants say "Wool, salt, iron. What have you got?" and "Silver by weight", and have
   nothing to trade. **Since the same evening** (the lore's handoff 30) they say why the stalls are bare, and that Old Aslak
   lives at Brynjasstad. The wall is as it was.
10. **Nothing points to Aslak's village.** The quest East closes on landing and the book is then empty. **Since the same
    evening** Dagnysnes' merchants name his village in their second line.
11. **The clues are buried.** A villager says one line a talk, and his clue is the fifth and sixth of seven. Twelve seconds
    pass between talks.
12. **The bag is full on day 4.** Thirty slots: ten tools and weapons and twenty kinds of thing, in stacks of 25.
13. **Flint weapons are a small step.** The flint spear does 1.05 against the stone spear's 0.94. The flint axe's worth is the
    old pines.
14. **The big log is never carried or towed.** It is split where it falls and the timber goes in the bag.
15. **Lines that no longer fit: replaced the same evening** (Alistair, LD's handoff 30). The troll in the hill cave and the
    raft you built are gone from home water; in their place a pair on Arnor's boat and a pair on Hallveig's blót. The
    merchants' "Wool, salt, iron" and "Silver by weight" are kept for chapters with trade.
16. **The Chapter 1 start has no sinew**, so the first lesson asks for a boar. That reads well; I left it.

## Questions for Robin

1. **Should the blót gate the ship?** Mine: yes. Aslak sells the sail lesson only once you have stood at the blót ("you are
   one of us now"). The main goal is then on the road to the end, as first decided on 2026-10-26.
2. **Should the ship need more than one island round?** Mine: yes, three. Keep flint on every beach, put the old pines on one
   island and the flax on another, and never both on the island nearest the landing.
3. **How hard should the Old Man be?** Mine: faster (60, still slower than a jog), and his blow harder to block. He should
   kill a careless player in full leather; today he cannot.
4. **Should you be able to build a butcher's table?** Mine: yes, at your home once the claim stone stands. It gives the claim
   stone a use and turns pelts from a chore into a hunt.
5. **How long should chapter 1 be?** One hour on a known path is short for the islands it has. If you want two to three, the
   levers are questions 1, 2 and 4, a dearer ship (salt for provisions, rope), and the wolf-skin gear asked for by the cold.
6. **Should the first village from the landing be Aslak's?** Mine: yes. Swap the two places, or give Dagnysnes a gate on the
   landing's side and a villager who says where Aslak lives.
7. **Should a villager's clue come first?** Mine: yes, his first line when he has a clue you have not heard.
8. **Should the night bite?** Today a bed removes it. Mine: leave it for now; decide with the wolf-skin gear and winter.
