# A pet: ideas to widen Robin's system (proposal, waiting for Robin, 2026-10-23)

**Decided so far (Robin, 2026-10-23):** the pet system is one of the big new systems a chapter opens with ("the game opening up with big
new systems over time"); which chapter is not yet said. It comes through **a family that is very close with animals and keeps a lot
of pets** (Robin will think more on them; who they are and where they live is Alistair, LD's to shape when he does). **Different kinds
of pets with different uses: yes. A pet is its own person, named and cared for: yes.** The rest below is still proposal.

Robin's seed (in `idea-bank.md`): a pet that follows beside you and idles round the base at home, with a command mode: a held
key turns the keys into pet orders, a yellow ring at the mouse, and whatever the ring points at lights up with the word for what
the pet would do there ("Attack" on an enemy). Everything below is mine to propose and Robin's to pick from; none of it is built.

## 1. Which animals, and how you get them

Each kind is a different game, not a skin. The first should be the dog; the rest come with the seas (one new animal a circle, as
the ores).

- **The hound** (the starter island, chapter 1). Thorgunna's dog survived the raid: a half-wild hound that keeps its distance from
  the steading, watches you from the trees and sleeps by the graves. Meat laid by your fire three evenings wins it; the third
  evening it eats from your hand and follows from then. No line explains it; Arnor has one remark ("That one was hers"). A thing
  found, not bought, as the game states no goals.
- **A pup from a village** (home water). Old Aslak's village has a bitch with pups; one costs a little, grows over a season into
  what its line is: a hound (the nose), a spitz (quick, loud, the herder) or a big grey farm dog (the bite, slow).
- **The wolf** (the first sea). The night pack has a den; kill the pack and there is a cub. A wolf is the better fighter, feared by
  men (a man facing a wolf fights worse), never quite a dog: it will not Stay long, and villagers hide from it, so it is no dog
  for a town at peace.
- **The raven** (the east, trade). A tamed raven is the explorer's pet: it flies ahead of the ship and the chart shows what it saw
  (an island's shape, a village, smoke); on land it sits on your shoulder and finds carrion (a kill). It fights nothing. A bird
  of the gods in the background, never named as that.
- **The cat** (a trader's rarity, the hub). A ship's cat: the stores at home keep better (a comfort point, a buff, never a meter)
  and it sleeps on the bed. No orders at all; it does what it likes, which is the joke.
- **The bear** (the north, late). The jarls kept tame bears; a cub from a den on a mountain island grows over two winters into the
  showpiece: a brute on your side that breaks a shield wall, that every village fears, that eats as much as three men. The law
  of the age fined a man whose bear did harm: a bear in a village at peace stirs it.

## 2. The orders (Robin's ring, filled in)

The ring at the mouse while the key is held; the word over what it points at; a click gives the order. The pet does one order at
a time and goes back to following when it is done.

| Pointing at | Word | What the pet does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a foe | **Attack** | goes for him; a dog bites the leg (a stagger, the man turns to it); a wolf holds on; a bear breaks the guard |
| the ground | **Stay** | sits there and guards: barks at a stranger within sight, bites one who comes at the spot |
| the ground, far | **Come** / **Go** | runs there and waits (the way to send it round a house, or away from a fight) |
| a drop | **Fetch** | brings it to you (one small thing at a time; a hound brings a felled bird, a hare) |
| a bush, reeds, tall grass | **Flush** | runs through and puts up what hides there (a hare, a bird, a boar) |
| a blood trail, a beast seen | **Track** | follows the scent and bays when it has it in sight; the bleeding deer found without the trail |
| a boar or a bear | **Hold** | grabs and holds the beast still two seconds, for your blow (the hound's reason to be) |
| the ground near a secret | **Dig** | scratches where something is buried: the midden cache, the hollow mound, the cellar under the rug (`V.secrets` is the register; the dog smells what is within a tile or two) |
| a man or a beast, in a place at peace | **Quiet** | no bark, no growl: the order before a quiet raid; a dog not told is the noise that wakes the place |
| your ship | **Guard the ship** | stays aboard on a raid and barks when men of the place come to take their loot back (`takeBackTick`), fights them on the sand |
| the hero (a long hold) | **Heel** | at your side through anything, no fighting: the walk through a town |

Not every animal takes every order (the raven takes Go, Come and Track; the cat none; the wolf no Fetch), which is how the kinds
differ without a stat screen.

## 3. What a pet is for

- **The hunt.** The dog is the best reason: it finds game (a nose line toward the nearest deer in the undergrowth), flushes,
  tracks and holds. With a hound the bow's deer hunt becomes a hunt and the boar fight a two-handed thing.
- **The fight against three** (`combat-crowd.md`). The dog is the fourth answer: one of the three turns to it, so you face two.
  A man bitten in the leg staggers, the chain's opening. The dog goes down as a friend does and gets up when it is over; it dies
  only as a hirdman dies, in battle, never at random (Robin's rule for the hird) and only if you leave it down on a raid's
  ground as the ship sails (lost, won back as a man is).
- **The night.** The wolves come each night; a dog by the fire growls before they are seen, fights beside you, and keeps them a
  little further off, as the fire does. A dog at home wakes you (a bark on the speech board) when something comes to the gate.
- **The raid.** Theirs and yours: a village keeps a dog (the idea bank has it), which smells you at the fence and barks the place
  awake unless it is dealt with first (meat thrown, a quiet kill, or your own dog fights it: a dog fight at the gate while the
  men sleep). Your dog on a raid is a danger and a tool: Quiet and Guard the ship are the orders that make it one.
- **Home.** It idles at the hearth, greets you at the gate, sleeps on the rug (a comfort point: the rested buff grows), begs at
  the table. A kennel piece on the Building board once you have a dog; a second dog stays home while one follows.
- **The sea.** The dog in the bow (the sagas have them on ships); the raven ahead of the ship over the water, back to the mast.
- **The seasons.** Its prints beside yours in the snow; a hound hunts best in autumn when the deer are fat; in winter it sleeps
  more and eats more; a pup born in spring is grown by autumn.

## 4. A pet is a person

The hird's roll gives the shape: **stats** (nose, bite, nerve, wits) and **traits** (loyal, a barker, a thief that steals from
the table, lazy, brave to a fault: it goes for the troll). Training as the hird's, by doing: a dog that has held ten boars holds
better. **Food is a reward:** meat in the evening makes it quick and brave the next day; unfed it is slow and sad, never gone
(it hunts a hare for itself now and then, and sometimes brings you one). **A name** from the lore's pools, given by you as the
hero's is. **A life**: a dog lives about ten winters of the game's year; it greys, slows, and one winter stays by the hearth; a
pup of its line is in the village that spring. No line says it; the Lore Book's page (Alistair, LD's words) does.
