In the run the hero set foot on six islands: the big island (an old pine), a flint beach, Saltvik, the flax and pine island,
his home, and the ruin's island for the Old Man (who is not needed for the ship).

## Totals for the way out

- Flint 10 (2 for the axe, 8 for the loom's parts): one beach gives 10 to 60.
- Wolf pelts 3 and a wolf's blood for the claim stone: wolves come at night on the big island, three or four at a time.
- Leather 8 (4 for the loom's parts, 4 for the ship): four boar or deer butchered, or wolves.
- Flax 24: one flax island has 30 to 500 plants.
- Old pines 2: the big island has at least two; the second can also come from an island and be towed home.
- Wood about 70, fibre about 15, stone 10.

## What changed after the first pass

The first pass asked 6 timber and 3 bolts, and 6 flint and 2 pelts for the loom's parts, and the mountain's foot held 8 to 26
old pines: the ship was a few minutes' work over two islands. Now: 12 timber and 4 bolts, 8 flint and 3 pelts, and only a few
old pines at the foot (never fewer than two). The run still needs no more than three islands for the ship itself (a flint
beach, a flax island, Saltvik): the others give the bow (elm), the cape (the Old Man), the drink (the mountain) and places to
live. If the ship should lean on more islands, the levers are: the mast from a towed log only (no old pine on the big island),
salt in the ship's provisions, or rope from a second plant.

## What the run does not measure

How long it takes by hand. The script is put where each thing is; a player rows and walks. A first guess, to be corrected by
Robin's play: flint and the axe 10 minutes with the row out, two pines and their splitting 10, wolves and boar for pelts and
leather two nights, Saltvik and the loom 15, flax 10, the pier, yard and ship 10: two to three hours after the blót.
