# Chapter 0's glimpses: a proposal (Clive, GD, 2026-10-27; waiting for Robin)

`docs/chapters.md` gives each chapter **glimpses**: small things the player sees, hears or finds that hint at what is coming,
without explaining anything (Robin's rule: the game states no goals). Chapter 0's are mine to propose. These are the options, with
what each hints at and who makes it. I recommend the four marked ★. Pick any.

| # | The glimpse | What it hints at | Who makes it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 ★ | **Smoke over the sea at dusk.** From the starter island's east shore, a thin column of smoke rises at the screen's east edge each evening, where Old Aslak's village lies. It is gone by night. | The big island and its people; the way out. | Clive, GD (drawn in code, like the lettering) |
| 2 ★ | **A sail passing far out.** Once or twice a day, a karve with a striped sail crosses the sea far off the starter island and is gone. | Ships, the wider sea, raiders like Ormr. | Clive, GD (the game's own karve, drawn small and far; the living sea has the room) |
| 3 ★ | **Wolves howling across the water.** At night, from the east, now and then, a far howl. No wolves on the island. | Chapter 1's threat, the wolves at night. | Derek, SD (a far howl), Clive, GD (when it plays) |
| 4 ★ | **Ormr's mark in the ashes.** In the burnt hall's ashes lies a broken shield painted with a serpent, his crew's. E on it: "Ormr's crew carried this sign." One line, no story. | Ormr Midgardbane as a real enemy, met later. | Colin, AD (the broken shield), Alistair, LD (the line and whether the serpent is his sign) |
| 5 | **An old offering place.** Near the graves, a low ring of mossy stones with a cracked bowl in the middle, long unused. Nothing happens there. | The blót and the altar, met at the village's rite in chapter 1. | Colin, AD (the piece) |
| 6 | **Green in a stone.** One rock by the steading shows green streaks; struck, it gives stone, and Arnor says, once: "Copper. Not here, though. Further out." | Copper and smithing, later chapters. | Alistair, LD (the line), Colin, AD (the rock's look) |
| 7 | **A broken rod at the steading.** A snapped fishing rod and a tangled line by the well. | Fishing, taught by the fisherman in chapter 1. | Colin, AD (the piece) |
| 8 | **Arnor speaks of the village's rite.** Once, after the meat, Arnor mentions that the village across the water holds a blót when summer turns. | The village's rite and the runes. | Alistair, LD (the line) |

**Why these four:** they are seen or heard without anything to read (1, 2, 3), and one is found (4), so they keep to "everything is
found". Each points at a different part of what comes: the big island, the sea and ships, the wolves, and the enemy behind the
burnt steading. Options 5 to 8 are fine too, but they lean on Arnor's words or on a piece nobody can use yet.

**What each needs before it can be built:** 1 and 2 only code (mine). 3 needs a sound from Derek, SD. 4 needs a piece from Colin, AD
and a line from Alistair, LD, who would also say whether a serpent is Ormr's sign.
