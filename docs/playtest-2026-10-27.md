## Broken, and fixed

1. **A fall sent the hero back to the starter island, stranded.** He always woke at Arnor's straw bed, even after five nights in his
   own bed on Selsey, and his only boat was on Selsey. He now wakes by the bed he last slept in, if it still stands
   (`seen.bedAt`, `toSpawn`). After that, Arnor's straw bed, then the wreck. Checked: he drowned off Selsey and woke by his own bed.
2. **A stone altar built from the Building board never worked.** No beast could be laid on it ("Set it down"), because a built
   altar never got the flag the game looks for. Only the Rune Editor's altar, made by hand, had it. It now counts by its name, so
   older saves' altars work too.
3. **Drowning never killed.** Out of stamina in deep water, his health fell to 0 and he swam on forever at 0. Now he drowns and
   wakes as from any fall (the engine's new `api.die`).
4. **Arnor's gift boat lay with her bow on the sand,** so W did nothing until you thought to press S. She also had no name
   ("Rowboat"). She now floats with her bow to the open water and takes a name from the lore's pool.
5. **The altar board:**
   - It still said the actives are "charged on the blood you draw", and a rune's card said "Wakes on blood". Both now give the
     stamina cost (stamina only since 2026-10-25).
   - That line sat under the plaque. It now sits below it.
   - The eleven slots ran off the bottom of the board, with "E or Esc closes" over the Pet row. The rows fit now, and the pet's
     slot is hidden until a pet rune exists.
6. **The hero was not drawn while butchering** until his knife and work art had loaded (in real time he shows after a moment). That
   art is now loaded while he carries a beast, before he reaches the table.
7. **Arnor's waiting line** went back to "walls and a roof" for a moment once the walls and fire were done, before the quest closed.
   Its order is fixed: walls, then fire, then meat.
8. **A dead recipe** (`arrows5`, an old arrow recipe the game always hid) is gone.

## Questions for Robin (play, not bugs)

1. **No butcher's table off the starter island.** Arnor's is the only one, and none can be built. So on the big island a killed boar,
   deer or moose gives nothing unless it's carried across the sea. Leather comes only from butchering, so the quiver and the rug wait
   on that too. My proposal: a butcher's table on the Building board off the starter island (6 wood and 2 stone, mine). Or should a
   village have one?
2. **The Wooden Sword:**
   - A young boar took 10 blows. With the guard up that was 5 s and 3 health lost. Swinging without guarding it took 34 s and cost
     53 health.
   - A grown boar took 19 blows. With the guard up that was 14 s and 18 health lost. Without guarding it killed the hero.
   - It's fine for a player who guards, and hard for one who doesn't. Keep it?
3. **Nothing says to sleep for the boat.** After the meat, Arnor says nothing while he mends. The player has to guess that a night
   passes before the boat is ready. Should Arnor say a line here, like "She'll be ready by morning" (a request to Alistair, LD)?
4. **Arnor's "That's a home again. First one here in two winters."** comes at the boat the next morning, not when the house is
   mended. It belongs to his `teach` entry. Should it be spoken earlier, when the house quest closes?
5. **Free building on the big island is open at once.** The night slept under the straw bed's roof opens the whole Building board
   (key `slept`), so on Selsey you can build anything. Your plan for chapter 1 has Arnor teach free building at your home. Should the
   walls wait for that?
6. **The scarecrow and the shield rack** are still on the Building board, though they were taken out of the steading as not a Viking
   farm's. Remove them from the board too?
7. **The first blót's offer was random** (the Overhead and the Long leap). Your plan's scripted first pick (chapter 1) isn't built yet.
8. **A beast on your shoulders is not saved:** reload while carrying one and it is gone. Keep it that way?
9. **Changing weapons mid-fight:** pressing a number while swinging or guarding did nothing for my bot (the cudgel never came out).
   Is that what you want, or should the swap wait and happen when the swing ends?
10. **The altar against the house wall:** I built it 93 units from the fire, against the house's south wall. The game allowed it, and
    the hero did the rite with his back to the wall. Fine, or should an altar want open ground behind it?
