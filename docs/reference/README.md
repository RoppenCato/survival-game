in `docs/art-direction.md` once it is written. This folder holds the public-domain references, fetched from Wikimedia Commons and
kept at 1200 px; `_board.jpg` is a contact sheet of them. Egerkrans is a living artist, so his pictures are **linked, never copied**.

## What to take from each

- **Egerkrans:** the line (confident, varying weight, thick on the shadow side, thin toward the light), the strong silhouette, flat
  two-tone fills with grain inside the shape, a limited palette per picture with one hot accent, poses with weight, figures small
  and lean with character in the face and the gear.
- **Bauer:** one warm sun and cool blue-grey shadows, ochre and moss and bone, the forest deep and old behind the open ground,
  mist low in the valleys, small people under big trolls and bigger trees, the trolls' knobbed hands, long noses, beads and rings.
- **Kittelsen:** the trolls that are half landscape (moss, trees and rock growing on them), the night pictures, the näcken in the tarn.
