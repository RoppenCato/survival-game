# Egerkrans, from Robin's copy of *Nordiska gudar* (2026-10-07)

Six photographs Robin took of pages in his own copy of Johan Egerkrans's *Nordiska gudar*. They are study copies of a living
artist's work, so they **stay on this machine**: the folder's pictures are in `.gitignore` and are never pushed. The notes
below are what we take from them; the notes are in the repo.

| File | Page | What to take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hel-and-garm.jpg` | Hel with the skull, the hound behind | A whole picture in one cold grey-green; the one warm accent (the gold brooch, the ember lights at the left edge); the hound as a silhouette with a lit eye and a lit row of teeth; the chain as a drawn ornament |
| `thor-fishing-jormungandr.jpg` | Thor and Hymir in the boat, the serpent below | Water as flat bands of tone with painted highlights; depth by fading to the paper; the serpent huge and dim behind, the boat small and sharp; one picture, two values of light |
| `tyr-and-fenrir.jpg` | Tyr's hand in Fenrir's mouth | The line: thick on the shadow side, thin toward the light; fur as a jagged silhouette, not strands; the wolf's orange eyes the only hot colour; the man lean and long with his gear drawn with love (the belt plates, the runes) |
| `warrior-with-shield.jpg` | A warrior with the shield and the sword over the shoulder | Gold ornament as the highlight on brown and grey; fur, cloth and iron each with its own texture; the paper's grain showing through every fill; the cloak torn at the hem |
| `thor-on-the-rock.jpg` | Thor on the rock with the hammer | A warm picture: ochre sky, red hair as the accent; the cloak as one dark shape with a textured edge; the rock drawn with hatching; a figure on a landmark, seen from below |
| `odin-with-gungnir.jpg` | Odin with the ravens and the spear | Knotwork faint in the background as pattern; a figure almost all dark with the face and hands lit; small detail everywhere in the gear (the ring, the brooches, the carved spear) |

**What all six share, and what we build toward:** a limited palette per picture with one hot accent; a dark confident line
that varies in weight; flat fills with grain; silhouettes first, detail second; lean figures with character in the face and
the gear; backgrounds as tone and faint pattern, never clutter.
